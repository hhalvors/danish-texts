The supplementary volume: seventeen papers Bohr did not collect himself,
several never before printed in Danish, gathered by Steen Brock for
Philosophia and by Jan Faye and Henry Folse for Ox Bow. It includes the 1935
*Physical Review* reply to Einstein, Podolsky and Rosen — with their paper as
an appendix — the Faraday Lecture, and the 1950 Open Letter to the UN.

## The text

Eight volumes are planned, four Danish and four English; this is volume IV
of each. The Danish follows *Filosofiske Skrifter*, Bind IV (Forlaget
Philosophia, København 2013, with the Niels Bohr Arkiv); the English follows
Ed. Jan Faye and Henry J. Folse. Woodbridge, Conn.: Ox Bow Press, 1998.

## Editorial note

This edition's one substantive addition to the existing printings is
**dual marginal pagination**. Bohr is normally cited from the collected
volumes — "PWNB IV, p. 74" — but the arguments were first made in
journals, congress proceedings and Festschriften, and it is that pagination the
contemporary correspondence and the *Niels Bohr Collected Works* cite. A reader
working between the two has had to convert by hand. Here both are marked:

- **`[n]`**, bold, in the **left** margin — page *n* of the **original**
  publication named in that essay's *First published* line above.
- **`|n|`**, italic, in the **right** margin — page *n* of the **collected
  volume**: Ox Bow Press in English, Philosophia in Danish.

Markers sit at the exact word the page turns on. They are set with `marginnote`
rather than `\marginpar` so that they survive inside block quotations, lists and
footnotes, where `\marginpar` silently drops them. Because the collected editions
reset the type, the two series interleave irregularly; that is the point.

Transcription follows the house rule for this repository: the text is
diplomatic. Printer's errors are set as printed and logged in a `%` comment at
the site, never silently corrected.

## Rights

Unlike everything else in this collection, these texts are **not public
domain**. Bohr died in 1962; the Danish edition (Philosophia 2013) and the
English (Ox Bow Press) are both in copyright. Permission to publish has been
obtained, and this volume is live on the site. The permission covers the eight
volumes described here; further Bohr material — correspondence, the
*Collected Works*, the unpublished manuscripts in the Niels Bohr Arkiv — is not
covered by it and should not be added without checking.
