# Poul Martin Møller — *Efterladte Skrifter*: priority list of philosophical pieces

A survey of all six volumes (3rd ed., C.A. Reitzel), ranked by how much each
piece reveals about **what kind of philosopher Møller was**. Page numbers are the
printed pages within each volume; scans are the three two-volume PDFs in
`~/bibliotek/Møller, Poul Martin/` (`efterladte-skrifter1.pdf` = vols 1–2,
`efterladte-skrifter3.pdf` = vols 3–4, `efterladte-skrifter5.pdf` = vols 5–6).

Status tags reflect this repo's `catalog.yaml`: **done**, **in progress**,
**to do**.

---

## What the corpus is (volume map)

- **Vol 1** — Lyric poems and the verse translation of the *Odyssey*. *Literary.*
- **Vol 2** — *En dansk Students Eventyr* (novella) + *Anhang*; the Olsebymagle
  statistical sketch; *Et Himmelbrev*; *Optegnelser fra Chinareisen*; *De
  opdigtede Historier*. *Mostly literary; one philosophically loaded episode.*
- **Vol 3** — *Strøtanker* (aphorisms, three date-groups) + six *Brudstykker*
  (fragments). *Core philosophical material.*
- **Vol 4** — *Udkast til Forelæsninger over den ældre Philosophies Historie*
  (lectures on the history of ancient philosophy), with the *Indledning* =
  "Hvad er Philosophie?". *His metaphilosophy + reading of the ancients.*
- **Vol 5** — *Studier, Afhandlinger og Recensioner af philosophisk Indhold.*
  *The single most philosophical volume.*
- **Vol 6** — *Recensioner af æsthetisk Indhold* + *Poul Martin Møllers Levnet*
  by F. C. Olsen. *Aesthetics + the biography (essential context, not by M.).*

Møller's philosophical signature — against which "value" is judged below: an
**anti-speculative, personalist, psychologically acute "philosophy of life"** that
sets the concrete existing, morally responsible *person* against Hegelian
system-building; prizes clarity and common sense; is nourished by the ancients
(Socrates, Aristotle); and turns on concepts like **personality, immortality,
irony, and affectation (inauthenticity)**. He is the decisive figure just
upstream of Kierkegaard (who dedicated *The Concept of Anxiety* to his memory).

---

## Tier 1 — Indispensable (the heart of his philosophy)

1. **Om Muligheden af Beviser for Menneskets Udødelighed** — vol 5, pp. 38–140.
   *In progress* (repo `udoedelighed`, from the 1837 *Maanedsskrift*; vol 5
   reprints it). His **most important philosophical work**: an existential,
   personalist critique of speculative proofs of immortality, arguing that
   immortality is inseparable from the moral life of a concrete personality. The
   text that most shaped Kierkegaard. *Finish this first.*

2. **Ontologien eller Kategoriernes System** — vol 3 (Brudstykke V), pp. 189–212.
   **Done.** His systematic metaphysics in miniature: works through Kant, Schelling,
   and Hegel yet insists that *personality* is "actuality thought in its truth."
   Shows the metaphysical backbone under the existential themes.

3. **Strøtanker** — vol 3, pp. 1–147 (groups 1819–21, 1822–26, 1826–37).
   *Translated* from a modern e-book (repo `stroetanker`); **no scholarly
   transcription from the Fraktur original yet.** The richest single source for his
   temperament and range: existential psychology, philosophy of religion,
   aesthetics, method, and cultural criticism. High value; worth a proper
   transcription from vol 3.

## Tier 2 — Major, still largely untouched

4. **Forelæsnings-Paragrapher over Moralphilosophien** — vol 5, pp. 141–164.
   *To do.* His **ethics** in systematic numbered paragraphs — the practical-
   philosophy counterpart to *Ontologien*. Central and currently missing.

5. **Om Begrebet Ironie** — vol 3 (Brudstykke II), pp. 152–158. *To do.*
   Short (~7 pp.). Irony as a philosophical/existential category — a direct line to
   Kierkegaard's *Concept of Irony*. Excellent, self-contained next transcription.

6. **Forberedelser til en Afhandling om Affectation** — vol 3 (Brudstykke IV),
   pp. 163–188. *To do.* "Affectation" (inauthenticity, self-deception) is one of
   Møller's **signature ethical-psychological concepts** and his sharpest tool of
   cultural diagnosis. Pairs naturally with the *Strøtanker*.

7. **Forelæsninger over den ældre Philosophies Historie** (whole course) — vol 4.
   *Indledning ("Hvad er Philosophie?") in progress* (repo `hvad-er-philosophie`,
   from the Madsen ed.); the rest *to do*. His **metaphilosophy** and his readings
   of Socrates, Plato, and especially Aristotle ("Logik eller Metaphysik") reveal
   how historically grounded and anti-systematic his conception of philosophy was.
   Large; best taken in sections (start by finishing the Indledning from the vol 4
   scan, then Socrates, then Aristotle).

## Tier 3 — Valuable supporting texts

8. **Nogle Betragtninger over populære Ideers Udvikling** — vol 5, pp. 23–37.
   *To do.* His philosophy of culture / how ideas develop and popularize.

9. **The philosophical *Recensioner*** — vol 5: *Anmeldelse af Sibberns Logik*
   (p. 165), *Recension af L. Zeuthens "Om Philosophien og dens Dyrkelse"*
   (p. 178), *Recension af P. C. Kierkegaards "de notione atque turpitudine
   mendacii"* (p. 182, on lying), *Recension af Sibberns "Om Poesie og Konst"*
   (p. 201). *To do.* Møller in critical dialogue with his contemporaries; the
   P. C. Kierkegaard and Sibbern reviews are the most philosophically telling.

10. **En dansk Students Eventyr — the Licentiat episode** — vol 2 (also in vol 3).
    *Licentiaten done* (repo `students-eventyr`); remainder *to do*. Literary, but
    the *locus classicus* of Møller's diagnosis of **nihilism / paralytic
    inertia** — philosophically important out of proportion to its length.

11. **Shorter Studier** — vol 5: *Den philosophiske Sminkedaase* (p. 18), *Om
    Forceringer* (p. 19), *Om at fortælle Børn Eventyr* (p. 14). *To do.* Brief,
    satirical/critical pieces that show his style and cultural criticism.
    (*Qvindelighed*, p. 5, is **done**.)

## Tier 4 — Context / lower philosophical yield

12. **Ahasverus** — vol 3 (Brudstykke III), p. 159; **Om Sjælen (Oversættelse)** —
    vol 3 (Brudstykke VI), p. 213; **Noget om en didaktisk Logiks Foredrag** — vol 3
    (Brudstykke I), p. 149. *To do.* *Ahasverus* is a philosophy-of-life fragment;
    *Om Sjælen* is a translation (less original); the logic fragment is slight.

13. **Poul Martin Møllers Levnet** by F. C. Olsen — vol 6. The **biography** —
    indispensable *context* for reading everything above, but not by Møller.

14. **Vol 6 aesthetic reviews** (Hauch's *Bajazet og Tiber*, Heiberg's
    *Extremerne*, Wilster's *Iliad*, etc.) and **vols 1–2** (poems, *Odyssey*,
    travel prose). Illuminate his aesthetics and sensibility; peripheral to his
    philosophy proper.

## Recommended working order

1. **Finish *Udødelighed*** (Tier 1 #1) — the keystone, already underway.
2. **Om Begrebet Ironie** (Tier 2 #5) — short, high-value, self-contained.
3. **Forelæsnings-Paragrapher over Moralphilosophien** (Tier 2 #4) — fills the
   ethics gap next to the now-complete *Ontologien*.
4. **Om Begrebet Affectation** (Tier 2 #6), then a **Fraktur transcription of the
   Strøtanker** (Tier 1 #3).
5. Then the **vol 4 history-of-philosophy course** in sections, and the **vol 5
   Recensioner**.
