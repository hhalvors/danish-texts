Harald Høffding's *Erindringer* (Copenhagen: Gyldendalske Boghandel, Nordisk
Forlag, 1928; 324 pp. and an Indhold) is his account of his life from his
childhood to 1924, in eight chapters named only by their years: I. 1843–1861,
II. 1861–1874, III. 1874–1883, IV. 1883–1894, V. 1894–1904, VI. 1904–1914,
VII. 1914–1922, VIII. 1922–1924. A note printed beneath the Indhold says that
chapters I–VI were written in the winter of 1915–16, VII in 1922 and VIII in
the spring of 1924, and that a read-through before printing added some
parentheses and notes. Chapter II covers the student years and the press
discussion of winter 1865–66 over Rasmus Nielsen's doctrine of faith and
knowledge, which ties the book to the Nielsen, Brøchner and Brandes texts in
this collection.

## The text

A diplomatic transcription from the page images of the 1928 first printing,
of these sections only:

- **pp. 39–51** (chapter II): the student years and the teachers.
- **pp. 55–69** (chapter II): the discussion of 1865–66, which opens at the
  head of p. 59, and the run of letters that follows it, from 30 September
  1866 to 26 June 1870 (pp. 63–69).
- **pp. 77–82** (chapter II): Brøchner's last years and death.
- **pp. 90–95** (chapters II–III): Brandes; the end of chapter II and the
  first pages of chapter III.
- **The Indhold** and the note beneath it.

**The rest of the book is still to be done.** The transcription carries the
whole book as a skeleton, each chapter under its printed head, and every
stretch not yet transcribed is marked in the PDF with a line such as
"[pp. 52–54 not yet transcribed]". The transcribed ranges begin and end where
the pages do, so some open or close in mid-sentence or mid-word; the file
says so at each edge.

There is no translation of this edition yet.

**A withdrawn predecessor.** Until September 2026 this page offered a text of
selected paragraphs of chapter II, with an English translation, taken from
the 2021 Lindhardt og Ringhof e-book of the 1928 text rather than from the
page, and carrying page numbers that the paginationless e-book could not
supply. Both have been withdrawn. The present text was made from scratch at
the scan, and neither the e-book nor that text was consulted in making or
checking it.

## Editorial note

**Source.** A scan of the 1928 first printing ("Oplag: 2000 Eksemplarer",
Gyldendals Forlagstrykkeri) from Slægtsforskernes Bibliotek (Danskernes
Historie Online), 327 PDF pages with an ABBYY text layer.

**Page map.** Printed p. N = PDF page N + 2, uniform through the book;
measured from 216 of the scan's own folios and read at the image on every
page transcribed. The Indhold is on an unnumbered page after p. 324, cited
here as [325]. Page numbers in the margin of the PDF are printed pages, each
placed at the word where the page begins, and all of them were read at the
image. The chapter IV figure in the Indhold is 121 (the text layer's "I2J"
is a damaged 1).

**Conventions.** The book is set in antiqua with old-style figures. Its
emphasis is italic, rendered as italic. Orthography stands as printed.
Footnotes are marked "1)" and numbered afresh on each page, as in the book.
The quoted letters of pp. 63–69 are set, as in the book, as ordinary
paragraphs without quotation marks. Letters in angle brackets ⟨ ⟩ are
supplied by the editor where the scan, not the print, has lost them. There
is one such place: p. 61, "⟨tæn⟩kte", at the damaged foot of the page, where
another copy should be checked.

**Printer's errors** are transcribed as printed and logged in the source:
p. 41 "andelig", and "Kierkegaard „Til Selvprøvelse“" without the genitive
s; p. 44 "sprængtes.Hinsidighedens"; p. 46 "mine legemlige Konstitution";
p. 59 "Rasmus Nielsens hørte"; p. 60 "dennne", "( som"; p. 63 "jeg ngen
Præst"; p. 78 "sine unge … Hustru", "Tillidt"; and in the Indhold "VI·".
The head of chapter VI (p. 224, not yet transcribed) prints "V".

**How the accuracy was established.** Each batch of pages was transcribed
from the image, and its words were diffed against the scan's text layer
before it went into the edition. Every one of the 41 pages was then read
again at the image, word by word and mark by mark, by readers who had not
transcribed it and who did not have the text layer. A further reader
transcribed four randomly drawn pages blind and compared them with the
edition token by token (1,626 tokens, no differences), and also settled the
marks the second readers could not decide. One mark was corrected: on p. 66
the comma after the italic *Kulturformer* is itself italic. No word errors
were found. The full record is in the comment header of `transcription.tex`.
