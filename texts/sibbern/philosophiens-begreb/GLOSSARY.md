# Glossary — Sibbern, *On the Concept, Nature, and Essence of Philosophy* (1843)

Working English equivalents for the load-bearing vocabulary, to keep the
translation consistent across sessions. Register: **lightly modernized**.
Convention: on a term's **first occurrence in each section**, gloss the Danish
in brackets, e.g. "fundamental cognition [Grunderkjendelse]"; thereafter use the
English alone. Update this file whenever a new term is fixed or a choice is revised.

**This is the companion volume to *Om Erkjendelse og Granskning* (1822).** Keep
shared terms consistent with `../erkjendelse/GLOSSARY.md` (the Fortale explicitly
relates the two books). The terms below are seeded from that glossary plus the
vocabulary specific to this *Propædeutik*.
