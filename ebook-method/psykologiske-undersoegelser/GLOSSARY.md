# Glossary — Høffding, *Psykologiske Undersøgelser* (1889) → English

Settled 2026-10-09 by Claude without a round of approval (Hans: "go straight on through to the end"); the choices
marked ◆ are the ones to look at (also listed in the book's RESUME-NOTES).  Binding for the translators
(TRANSLATOR-BRIEF.md).  Counts are occurrences in transcription.tex.  Precedent: the repo's English of Høffding's
*Psykologi i Omrids* (texts/hoeffding/psykologi/translation.tex), which this treatise continues and cites ("min
Psykologi"): Forestilling = idea, Fornemmelse = sensation, sjælelig = psychical, Opmærksomhed = attention,
Drift = impulse, Selviagttagelse = self-observation.  Rule: one Danish term, one English term, wherever the sense
allows; capitals as in ordinary English (Danish capitalizes every noun).

## ◆ Choices to look at

| # | Danish | Chosen | Alternative | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Forestilling (401) | **idea**; Forestillingernes Association / Forestillingsassociation = **the association of ideas**; Ordforestilling = word-idea | presentation; representation | The English psychology of Høffding's day (and the 1891 English *Outlines*) says "association of ideas"; the repo's *Psykologi* uses idea throughout. |
| 2 | sjælelig (87), Sjæleliv, Sjæl | **psychical**; Sjælelivet = **psychical life**; Sjælen = the soul; aandelig = **mental** (the less technical word, pp. 96 ff.) | mental | The repo's *Psykologi* (psychical life 45×). |
| 3 | Bekendthedskvalitet (18) | **the quality of familiarity**; Bekendthed = familiarity; bekendt = familiar (known) | familiarity-quality | Høffding's coinage (p. 9: „vil jeg ... kalde Bekendthedskvaliteten“): an English phrase rather than a German-style compound. |
| 4 | Berøringsassociation (71) / Lighedsassociation (109) | **association by contiguity** / **association by similarity**; den frie Lighedsassociation = **free association by similarity**; Berøringsteorien = the contiguity theory; Berøringsforhold = relation of contiguity | contiguity-association | The standard English names (Hamilton, Bain, Ward, whom he cites). |
| 5 | Aktivitet (82) / Virksomhed (50) | both **activity**; Virksomhedstrangen = **the urge to activity**; Tankevirksomhed = activity of thought; Virksomhed in the sense „Arbejde“ = work | Virksomhed = action | Høffding uses the two as one concept (ch. V defines Aktivitet and goes on with Virksomhed); a second English word would suggest a distinction he does not draw. |
| 6 | Dækningslighed (8) / Ensartethed (5) / Analogi | **congruence** / **homogeneity** / **analogy**; kvalitetslig = qualitatively alike; forholdslig = alike in relation | coincidence-likeness; sameness of kind | p. 43: the grades of likeness, Ensheden eller Dækningsligheden = identity or congruence; Ensartethed og Analogi = homogeneity and analogy. |
| 7 | Medøvelse (1, p. 37) | **co-practice** | co-exercise | Coinage („som her med Rette er bleven kaldt Medøvelse“); Øvelse = practice throughout (Øvelsens Lov = the law of practice). |
| 8 | Anspændelse (11) | **exertion** (Anspændelse af Opmærksomheden = exertion of attention; anspænde sig = exert oneself) | tension; strain | Maine de Biran's effort, which Høffding glosses „(effort)“ (p. 68). |

## Headings and front matter

- Title: **Psychological Investigations.** / By / Harald Høffding.  The series line „Vidensk. Selsk. Skr., 6. Række,
  historisk og philosophisk Afd. III. 1.“ and the imprint („Kjøbenhavn. / Bianco Lunos Kgl. Hof-Bogtrykkeri (F.
  Dreyer). / 1889.“) stay as printed (bibliographic).  Indhold = **Contents**; Side = **Page**.
- Indledning. = **Introduction.**
- I. Umiddelbar Genkendelse. = **I. Immediate Recognition.**
- II. Berøringsassociationens Forudsætninger. = **II. The Presuppositions of Association by Contiguity.**
- III. Den frie Lighedsassociation. = **III. Free Association by Similarity.**
- IV. Forestillingsassociation og Sammenligning. = **IV. The Association of Ideas and Comparison.**
- V. Om Begrebet sjælelig Aktivitet. = **V. On the Concept of Psychical Activity.**
- VI. Sjælelig og fysisk Aktivitet. = **VI. Psychical and Physical Activity.**
- VII. Den sjælelige Aktivitets Lovmæssighed. = **VII. The Conformity to Law of Psychical Activity.**
- The numbered paragraphs 1.–42. keep their numbers; sub-points a. b. … and a) b) … as printed.
