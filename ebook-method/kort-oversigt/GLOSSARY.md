# Glossary — Høffding, *Kort Oversigt over den nyere Filosofis Historie* (4th ed., 1910) → English

Made 2026-10-17.  Binding for the translators (TRANSLATOR-BRIEF.md).  The book is a short textbook version of *Den
nyere Filosofis Historie*, so its terms are that book's: **ebook-method/nyere-filosofis-historie/GLOSSARY.md binds in
full** (its "Choices", "Terms" and "Abbreviations" sections; read them, the copy you are given is beside this file as
NYERE-GLOSSARY.md).  This file adds only what is particular to the *Kort Oversigt*: the title (Hans, 2026-10-17), the
four problems of the Introduction, and every heading.  American spelling.

## Headings (use exactly these)

- `\introhead{INDLEDNING.}` → `\introhead{INTRODUCTION.}`; `\sectionhead{Filosofiske Problemer.}` → `\sectionhead{Philosophical Problems.}`
- `\chapterhead{I.}{DEN NYERE FILOSOFIS TILBLIVEN.}` → `\chapterhead{I.}{THE ORIGIN OF MODERN PHILOSOPHY.}`
  - 1. Opdagelsen af det naturlige Menneske. → 1. The Discovery of Natural Man.
  - 2. Det nye Verdensbillede. → 2. The New Picture of the World.
  - 3. Den nye Videnskab. → 3. The New Science.
- II. SYSTEMDANNELSER. → II. THE FORMATION OF SYSTEMS.
- III. ERFARINGSFILOSOFIEN. → III. THE PHILOSOPHY OF EXPERIENCE.
- IV. OPLYSNINGSFILOSOFIEN. → IV. THE PHILOSOPHY OF THE ENLIGHTENMENT.
- V. IMMANUEL KANT OG DEN KRITISKE FILOSOFI. → V. IMMANUEL KANT AND THE CRITICAL PHILOSOPHY.
  - run in: 1. *Erkendelsesteori.* → 1. *Epistemology.*; 2. *Etik.* → 2. *Ethics.*; 3. *Enhedslære paa æstetisk og
    biologisk Grundlag.* → 3. *Theory of Unity on an Aesthetic and Biological Basis.* (the Kant titles in parentheses
    after them stay German)
- VI. ROMANTIKENS FILOSOFI. → VI. THE PHILOSOPHY OF ROMANTICISM.
- VII. POSITIVISMEN. → VII. POSITIVISM.
- `\chapterheadone{VIII. NYERE PROBLEMBEHANDLINGER.}{Nyere Problembehandlinger}` →
  `\chapterheadone{VIII. RECENT TREATMENTS OF PROBLEMS.}{Recent Treatments of Problems}`
  - a. Det psykologiske Problem. → a. The Psychological Problem.
  - b. Tilværelsesproblemet. → b. The Problem of Existence.
  - c. Erkendelsesproblemet. → c. The Problem of Knowledge.
  - d. Vurderingsproblemet. → d. The Problem of Valuation.
  - `\medskip\noindent Tillæg. \textbf{Aarsagssætningens Historie i den nyere Tid.}` →
    `\medskip\noindent Appendix. \textbf{The History of the Causal Principle in Modern Times.}`
- Names in headings and run-in openings: as printed (Réné Descartes, Darvin in the Contents) — the translation keeps
  the print's forms, as the transcription does.  (Unlike the big book's headings, which the big glossary normalizes.)
