You read sheets cut from a scan of a Danish book printed in 1910 (antiqua; old orthography: aa for å, capitalised nouns,
both „…“ and «…» quotes).  Each crop is labelled `s1-NN-KK  p.N`; its QUESTION is in the list below.  Pages given as
p.0 or below are front matter (PDF page = p + 10); p.96–99 are leaves after the last text page.  Answer every crop from
what is PRINTED: give misprints as printed, do not correct; ignore pencil marks and library stamps except to say what
printed mark lies under them.  Transcriptions: exact letters, accents, capitals and punctuation; `/` for a line break;
*word* for italic; [word] for SMALL CAPITALS (capital-height first letter, the rest small capitals); s p a c e d for
letterspacing.  Names at the start of paragraphs are often set in small capitals: look closely at each word of a name
(first names, initials and surnames may differ).
